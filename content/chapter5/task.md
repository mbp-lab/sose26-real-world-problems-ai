# Chapter 5 — Explainable AI - assignment


The following three stations will walk you through three high-stake situations featuring hypothetical AI decisions and explanations. 

Please read them carefully, study the accompanying explanations and answer the questions to each. We will use the tutorial session to dive deeper into the technical aspects. 



### The scenario

Imagine a day in your life a few years from now, where AI systems are responsible for many of the decisions that affect your daily life. In the morning, you receive the results of a medical examination. Around midday, you are informed that your loan application has been reviewed. In the evening, you receive the outcome of a job application. In each case, the AI system not only makes a decision but also provides an explanation for it.
